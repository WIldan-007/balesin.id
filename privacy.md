# Privacy Policy — balesin.id
**Last updated: July 24, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to balesin.id. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our social media automation platform.

## 2. Data We Collect
- **Account Data**: email, name, profile picture (from Google login)
- **Social Media Data**: Instagram username, post metrics, follower count, engagement data
- **Usage Data**: automation flows created, reply logs, click tracking
- **Payment Data**: processed securely via Midtrans (we do not store credit card details)

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- Provide automation features (auto reply, DM, comment tracking)
- Display analytics dashboard (engagement, follower growth)
- Process payments and manage subscriptions
- Improve platform performance

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **Firebase** (Google) — authentication & database
- **Meta Graph API** — Instagram data access
- **Midtrans** — payment processing
- **Google AI (Gemini)** — AI-powered reply generation

## 5. Data Security
We implement encryption, secure servers, and access controls to protect your data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 6. Your Rights
You can request data deletion at any time by contacting us. You can disconnect your Instagram/WhatsApp accounts from the Connections page.
